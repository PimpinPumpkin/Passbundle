PassBundle is installed

Three steps finish setting up the extension.

  1. Pin it. Open your browser's extensions menu and pin PassBundle to the toolbar.
  2. Sign in or create an account. Select the PassBundle icon. If your organization set up PassBundle for you, the server is already filled in. New accounts on our hosted service aren't open yet. To ask for one, see Contact.
  3. Bring your passwords. Import them from your browser or another password manager, or let PassBundle save them as you sign in to sites.

For anything else, see Help.
