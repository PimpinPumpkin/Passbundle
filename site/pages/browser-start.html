You're all set

The PassBundle extension is installed.

  1. Pin it. Open your browser's extensions menu and pin PassBundle to the toolbar.
  2. Sign in or create an account. Select the PassBundle icon. If your organization set up PassBundle for you, the server is already filled in.
  3. Bring your passwords. Import them from your browser or another password manager, or let PassBundle save them as you sign in to sites.
