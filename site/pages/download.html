Web vault

Your whole vault in any browser, on any computer, with nothing to install. Make your account here, or sign in.

Open the web vault

These are test builds. They're signed with PassBundle's own test keys, not yet by Apple or Microsoft, so the first time you open one, your computer asks you to allow it: on Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway; on a Mac, choose Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security; on Android, let your browser install apps when it asks. Each release lists its files' SHA-256.

Then bring your passwords

Export them from your browser or your old password manager, and import the file into PassBundle. Most people are done in a few minutes.

Step-by-step guides for 1Password, LastPass, and every browser

Run your own server

PassBundle is open source. Run the server on your own hardware and point the apps at it: the sign-in screen asks where your vault is.
