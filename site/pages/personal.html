Questions

What if I forget my master password?

We can't reset it, because we never have it. Name a legacy contact who can take over your vault: if you can't get in, they ask, and when the wait you chose is up, they set a new master password and pass it to you.

Is free really free?

Yes, with no limit on passwords or devices, no ads, and no trial that runs out. Premium and Families pay for extras, and businesses and MSPs pay per person.

Which devices does it work on?

The extension works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on Windows, Mac, and Linux, and goes into their stores at launch. There are test builds of the PassBundle app for Windows, Mac, Linux, and Android. The iPhone app is coming; until then, the web vault works in your phone's browser. See Download.

Can someone in my family see my own passwords?

No. Only what you put in a shared folder is shared. Your private vault is encrypted with your own key, and nobody in the family, including its owner, can open it.

Can I bring my passwords from another app?

Yes. Export them from your browser or your old password manager and import the file. See Switch to PassBundle.
