Questions

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. It has no time limit, no limit on passwords or devices, and no ads. Businesses and paid plans pay for it, and storing a vault costs us very little.

Can I run PassBundle on my own server instead?

Yes. PassBundle is open source, and the server runs on your own hardware at no cost. What you pay us for is the hosted service: we run it, back it up, keep it updated, and answer when you need help.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Business trials last {{prices.trialDays}} days without a card. Add one before the trial ends to keep going.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your vault stays yours. A Premium or Families account goes back to the free plan, with every password intact. You can export everything at any time.

Are prices in US dollars?

Yes, before any sales tax where it applies.
