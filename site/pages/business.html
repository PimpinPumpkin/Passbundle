Rollout

Every computer, in an afternoon.

Your organization's Settings page has an install command: one PowerShell line that sets up a Windows computer. Run it from Intune, group policy, or any RMM.

  • Installs the extension in Chrome and Edge, with your server already filled in
  • Turns off the browsers' own password managers, without deleting what's in them
  • Limit it to your email domains, so only your own people can use it
  • A whole office behind one internet connection can sign up at once
Policies

Rules that explain themselves.

Require two-step login, set master password strength, keep work accounts separate from personal ones, and decide whether people can export vaults. Every policy says in a sentence what it does.

  • Account recovery with automatic enrollment, on by default
  • Master passwords of at least 12 characters, checked for strength
  • Custom roles, for a manager who handles people but not policies
Microsoft Entra ID

Your directory does the joining and leaving.

Connect PassBundle to Microsoft Entra ID from your organization's settings. People you assign there are invited, people you turn off lose access, and Entra's groups become PassBundle groups with the same members.

  • Standard SCIM, set up in Entra in a few steps
  • Give a group a shared folder once, and everyone Entra adds to it gets it
  • Sign in with Entra Coming soon
Folders and permissions

Share by team, down to the capability.

Everything your company shares lives in its folders. Give a person or a group a preset, such as Can view or Can edit, or choose exactly what they can do in a grid.

  • Let a team see a folder's items without their passwords
  • A drop folder people can add to but not change
  • The Access page shows who reaches what, and how

Does it work on Macs?

Yes. The extension and the web vault work on Mac, Windows, and Linux, and there are test builds of the PassBundle app for all three. The rollout command and Windows Hello unlock are for Windows today; Mac rollout and Touch ID are next.

Is there an app for phones?

Android, yes: a test build that fills passwords in apps and browsers and unlocks with your fingerprint or face. The iPhone app is coming. Until then, people can open the web vault in their phone's browser.

Do you support single sign-on or directory sync?

Directory sync, yes: provisioning from Microsoft Entra ID adds, updates, and removes people and groups for you. Signing in with Entra is coming soon; until then, people sign in with their own master password.

Can we move from another password manager?

Yes. Import a shared export into your organization's folders, and each person brings their own passwords. See Switch to PassBundle.
