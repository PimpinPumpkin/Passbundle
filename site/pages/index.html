Autofill that gets it right

Logins, cards, and addresses filled in with a click. Right-click any field to choose what goes in it, or tell PassBundle never to fill it. A one-time code is never saved as your password.

Passkeys

Save passkeys as you create them and sign in with them in the browser, right next to your passwords.

A template for everything

Wi-Fi networks, software licenses, servers, routers, memberships, and more, with the right fields ready to fill. Make your own templates too.

Reminders before things expire

Give a passport, card, or license its expiration date, and PassBundle reminds you 30 days, a week, and a day before. The reminders come from your own device.

A health check for your passwords

See which passwords are weak, reused, or already in a known breach, and fix them one by one. Only a scrambled fragment of a password is ever checked, never the password.

Share safely with Send

Send a password or a note through an encrypted link that expires when you say. Add a password to the link, and limit how many times it opens.

Unlock with Windows Hello

On Windows, unlock PassBundle with your face, fingerprint, or PIN instead of typing your master password every time.
