How PassBundle keeps your vault safe

Questions

Is PassBundle free?

Yes. Every password on every device, autofill, passkeys, password health, and legacy contacts are free, with no ads and no trial that runs out. Paid plans start at launch. Then Premium ({{prices.premium}} a year) adds built-in two-step codes, file attachments, and file Sends, and Families ({{prices.families}} a year) gives up to {{prices.familiesPeople}} people Premium and shared folders. See Pricing.

What happens if PassBundle gets hacked?

Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key made from your master password, and our servers only ever hold the encrypted result. A stolen copy is useless without each person's master password, which we never have. See how PassBundle keeps your vault safe.

What if I forget my master password?

We can't reset it, because we never have it. Name a legacy contact who can take over your vault if you can't get in. At work, your organization can turn on account recovery, so an admin can reset it.

Does it work on my phone?

On Android, yes. A test build fills passwords and passkeys in apps and browsers, and unlocks with your fingerprint or face. The iPhone app is coming; until then, the web vault works in your phone's browser. See Download.

Can I bring my passwords from my browser or another app?

Yes. PassBundle walks you through your browser's own export and imports it, leaving out what you already have, and it imports from about 70 other apps, 1Password and LastPass among them. See Switch to PassBundle.

Do I need a domain or any IT setup?

No. An account takes any email address. Businesses don't need a domain, Active Directory, or an identity provider either; the install command works on any Windows computer, joined to a domain or not.

Can my family share passwords?

Yes. Families is {{prices.families}} a year for up to {{prices.familiesPeople}} people. It has shared folders for the bills, the Wi-Fi, and the streaming logins, and a private vault for each person that nobody else can open. See PassBundle for families.

Is PassBundle open source?

Yes. All of our code is public, so anyone can check what it does. The apps and the browser extension and web vault are under the GNU GPLv3, and the server is under the GNU AGPLv3. Run the server yourself, or use our hosted service once it opens at launch.
