# Before launching to companies

The work queue between now and selling PassBundle to MSPs and their clients. Background jobs take items from the top of "Next"; each lands as a pull request that merges when CI is green, after a security review when it touches permissions, keys, sign-in, or updates. The gap ranking comes from docs/parity.md.

- **Entra ID provisioning** over SCIM 2.0 (DESIGN.md, "Provisioning from Entra ID"). Checking it with a real tenant waits on a Microsoft 365 tenant.

Later: a notice on a closed phone (push), and single sign-on's trusted devices.

The desktop agent's own updater installs nothing until its update key is made (below).
- **Desktop and phone parity with the web vault**: folders and their permissions, templates, site icons, item history, password health (each login's strength everywhere; the Health list on computers only), and a Generator tab on phones, with the security review's fixes.
- **Web vault redesign** from the approved mockups: the menu as a card with jewel tiles, rows of site icons and tiles, the selected item in a side panel beside the list, and graphite glass, a neutral dark mode, for the web vault and the extension.
- **Exposed passwords in the MSP health view**, using the k-anonymity range check, with an off switch for the check after unlocking.
- **Events to security tools**: a signed webhook, syslog over TLS (JSON or CEF), and Microsoft Sentinel, per organization or one feed for an MSP.
- **Two-step login for admins** (DESIGN.md, "Two-step login for admins"): a client's owners, admins, custom roles that manage members, groups, shared folders, policies, or account recovery, and every MSP's staff sign in with an authenticator app, a security key or passkey, Duo, or the client's own single sign-on. On by default for the Business preset and every MSP.
- **Two-step login for everyone** (DESIGN.md, "Two-step login for everyone"): an organization, or an MSP's default, can require the same two-step login of every member, not only its admins; off by default. People no rule covers who have no two-step login at all get a nudge in the web vault, the extension, and the desktop and phone apps, and those whose only second step is email codes get one to add a stronger method; Not now hides either for 90 days on every device.
- **Item history and the new folders in the extension**: version history with restore, folders by owner, and see passwords, export, and share enforced on every extension path.
- **Command-line tool** (`passbundle`, docs/cli.md) for macOS, Linux, and Windows scripts, with folder permissions enforced on every command. Its release workflow is in this repository (`cli-release.yml`), and no release has been published yet: the first is one run you start, signed once the CLI release key is made (below).
- **Offboarding that holds** (DESIGN.md, "Offboarding"): removing an MSP, or one of its people, revokes what they made and holds; a client's own owners stay the client's; joining seals (version 2) and script grant seals (version 3) a hacked server can't steer.
- **Sign in with Entra (SSO) per organization** (DESIGN.md, "Sign in with Microsoft Entra ID"; setup in docs/sso-entra.md), tested against a stand-in for Entra. Checking it with a real tenant waits on a Microsoft 365 tenant. Later: trusted-device unlock, as its own design with test vectors.
- **Website redesign** in the new look (October 27): passbundle.com's pages in the apps' look, with the MSP story up front, on the preview address (https://preview.passbundle.pages.dev) until launch.
- **Joining settings a hacked server can't bring back** (November 6; DESIGN.md, "Joins a hacked server can't forge", version 3 joining seals): each browser remembers the newest joining setting it has seen for each organization and never goes back to an older one; and an admin confirmed before an organization required two-step login for admins, who never accepted the role, is revoked until they do. On the test server, once deployed, organizations whose joining seal is from before this wait for approval until someone saves how people join on the organization's Joining and security page.
- **Attachments in the apps** (November 9; DESIGN.md, "Attachments in the apps"): the desktop and phone apps attach files to items and delete attachments, in the web vault's format, with progress and Cancel, where the person may edit the item. Files up to 500 MB on a computer and 100 MB on a phone.
- **Send in the apps** (DESIGN.md, "Send in the apps"): the desktop and phone apps list, make, edit, and delete text Sends and copy their links, with a password, dates, a view limit, hidden email, and the organizations' Send policies honored; they list, edit, and delete file Sends made elsewhere. Making a file Send in the apps follows.
- **The pre-launch security pass** (October 27 to November 4) over the server, web vault, extension, apps, CI, and releases, with its fixes merged: connector pages that refuse other sites, PassBundle's own extension IDs, HSTS, the server's pass (DESIGN.md, "Running the server safely"), autofill, import, and extension hardening, offboarding and seal follow-ups, and Android vaults that autofill unlocked locking on time. ## Waiting on you

- **Production hosting**: a server; two storage buckets at a second provider (Backblaze or Cloudflare R2), one for backups and one for files, each with versioning on, so a backup or a file that's overwritten or deleted can still be got back; a backup key made on your own computer (ops/hosting/README.md, "The backup key"); a Cloudflare tunnel token; and the go-ahead to point vault.passbundle.com at the hosted stack.
- **Email and the domain**: a sending provider (Postmark, Amazon SES, or Resend) so invitations, verification and account recovery mail go out; and passbundle.com moved to PassBundle's own Cloudflare account (the nameservers are set at Namecheap), with Email Routing for hello@, privacy@, and security@ and the .io and .app redirects (site/README.md, "Moving to PassBundle's own Cloudflare account, with passbundle.com").
- **A Microsoft 365 tenant** (a free developer tenant is enough) to check Entra provisioning (built; the checks are in DESIGN.md, "Provisioning from Entra ID", "Status") and single sign-on (built; the checks are in docs/sso-entra.md, "Checking it with a real tenant") for real.
- **Apple Developer account** for an iPhone release; for filling in Safari with the iPhone app's AutoFill and passkeys, and any run on a real iPhone (both are built, and CI builds them and runs the app on a simulator); for security keys and YubiKey codes over NFC on iPhone; a signed and notarized Mac app and command-line tool; Apple's credential exchange import; and a Safari extension.
- **Release keys and the first releases**: the command-line tool's own Ed25519 key, made on your computer and added as the `PASSBUNDLE_CLI_RELEASE_KEY` secret, with its public key pinned in `cli-release.yml` (docs/cli.md, "The CLI release key", has the commands; until then a release carries SHA256SUMS and an unsigned manifest); the desktop agent's update key, made offline with minisign (passbundle-apps agent/README.md, "The update key"; until then the agent's own updater installs nothing); the go-ahead for the first command-line and extension releases, one workflow run each, started by hand (site/README.md, "Before launch"); and the server image's first release, a tag you push, after which its package's visibility on GitHub is yours to set (deploy/README.md, "Making a release").
- **Store listings and code signing** when you want them (you said not yet for the browser stores): Chrome, Edge, and Firefox listings; Mozilla's add-on keys (release Firefox installs only extensions Mozilla has signed); Google Play and App Store listings with the phone apps' store signing keys; and a Windows code-signing certificate, yours to choose and buy (DESIGN.md, "Desktop updates (Windows and Linux)", "Code signing, later").
- **Business details for the site and legal pages**: legal name, payment processor, server host, storage provider, refunds, governing law and venue, prices (marked as placeholders in site/).
- **A paid Have I Been Pwned key** for the report of breached accounts at a client's email domain: whether to buy one, which plan, and who verifies a client's domain.
- **MSP and Business billing**: Stripe and a seat's prices, which go in the hosted server's environment as `BILLING_SEAT_PRICES_MSP` and `BILLING_SEAT_PRICES_BUSINESS` (deploy/README.md, "Stripe"; the first build bills monthly prices only). What an invoice is based on, and which tier a month falls in, are decided (DESIGN.md, "Seats by client" and "Plans and billing").
- **Plans and billing**: thirteen choices about what each plan includes, what happens when a trial or a payment ends, and how the hosted service bills (DESIGN.md, "Plans and billing", "Decisions"; each has a pick). Enforcing plans can be built before any of them is made. Going live with payments needs a Stripe account and the prices.
- **The walk-through in Stripe's test mode** (a sandbox on your Stripe account, by hand, never part of a build), before payments go live. deploy/README.md, "Stripe", has each setting. In the sandbox:
  1. Make the two yearly prices, Premium and Families, and put their ids in `BILLING_PRICE_PREMIUM` and `BILLING_PRICE_FAMILIES`.
  2. Make a restricted key with Customers, Checkout Sessions, Customer portal, Subscriptions, Invoices, and Invoice items on Write, SetupIntents and PaymentMethods on Read, and everything else on None (`BILLING_API_KEY`; the server refuses the full secret key, so the test runs with the same permissions as live).
  3. Make the webhook endpoint at `/api/keykeeper/billing/events/stripe`: Snapshot payload, API version `2026-10-28.endive`, and the events `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `customer.subscription.paused`, `customer.subscription.resumed`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `invoice.marked_uncollectible`, `invoice.voided`, and `customer.updated`; its signing secret is `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
  4. Turn the customer portal on with payment method, invoice history, and canceling; switching plans, quantities, and pausing off.
  5. Set the retries (Billing, Revenue recovery, Retries) to end within 14 days, `PLAN_GRACE_DAYS`, and then cancel the subscription; and for the month's invoices (Settings, Billing, Invoices, Advanced invoicing features), retries of one-off invoices that end within the same 14 days. Stripe's documentation puts retrying a one-off invoice in Invoicing Plus: decide whether to have it, since without it a declined card is tried once and the pool stays in grace until someone pays in the portal.
  6. The invoice emails: receipts for successful payments (Settings, Business, Customer emails), an email when a card payment fails with the card updated in the portal (Settings, Billing, Subscriptions and emails), and Send finalized invoices to customers left off, since the month's invoice is charged to the card on file.
  7. Set `BILLING_SEAT_PRICES_MSP` and `BILLING_SEAT_PRICES_BUSINESS` to the monthly prices (the site's are `1:1.50,500:1.25,2500:1.00` and `1:2.00,250:1.75`), and leave `BILLING_INVOICE_SCHEDULE` at its default.
  8. Under Checkout and Payment Links, send customers who already have a subscription to the portal.
  9. Then, on a test server with `PLANS_ENFORCED` and `BILLING_PROVIDER=stripe`: pay for Premium and for Families with Stripe's test cards, see the plan change within seconds; cancel in the portal and see "ends on"; use a card that fails on renewal (a test clock moves the date) and see grace start; roll the signing secret with the old one kept, set `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET_OLD`, and see webhooks still taken; delete a paying account and a paying organization and see Stripe cancel both; add a card for an MSP's pool and for a Business organization, and after a month ends (a test clock can't move the server's month, so leave one running into the next) see one invoice each, with the Seats report's seat-days, paid from the card; and a declined card on that invoice putting the pool in grace.
  Live: the same in live mode, with a live restricted key, a new endpoint and its own signing secret, and live prices.
